Questions

Good to know.

What is a charan paduka puja box?

A charan paduka puja box is a presentation box holding sacred footprints (charan paduka), a deity panel and yantram or Shubh Labh plaques, finished in gold and silver plating. It is given as a blessing for festivals, weddings, housewarmings and new beginnings.

Which deities and designs do you offer?

Our collections include Lakshmi Ganesh, Lakshmi Saraswati Ganesh, Shrinathji, Radha Krishna, Kuber Lakshmi and many more, as well as a compact Yantram series. Download the catalogue for the full range.

Do you offer customised corporate gifts?

Yes. We offer bulk gifting, customisation, branding and personalised packaging for clients, employees, dealers and channel partners. Message us on WhatsApp or email us with your requirement.
