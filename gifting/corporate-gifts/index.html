Gifting guide

Corporate gifts that clients keep.

A festive corporate gift should be remembered after the season ends. Gold and silver plated puja boxes, with your company logo, are auspicious, respectful and easy to send to clients across India.

Why a puja box works as a corporate gift

Festive gifts for clients and teams are often sweets or dry fruits that are finished within days. A puja box is kept on display at home or in the office, so your brand stays visible long after the festival.

Lakshmi, Ganesh and Balaji are widely respected across communities in India, which makes the Corporate range a dependable choice for a mixed client list.

The Corporate range

Our Corporate series is made in the 9 × 4 × 5 inch box size, in four designs: Lakshmi Ganesh, Lakshmi Ganesh Saraswati, Balaji and D. Ganesh. You can also choose from the other collections for senior clients, or the compact yantram for large staff lists.

How bulk ordering works

  • Choose a design and order a paid sample to check the finish.
  • Share your logo on WhatsApp if you want it on the box.
  • Book your bulk order – minimum 120 pieces per design. Your sample cost is adjusted against the bulk rate.
  • Delivery across India, with GST and delivery charges applicable.

Plan early for festive dates

Festive orders with a logo take longer than ready stock. Place your sample order early, so that the logo preview and production do not clash with the festival rush.
