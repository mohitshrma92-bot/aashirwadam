Gifting guide

Diwali gift ideas that carry a blessing.

Diwali is the festival of Lakshmi and Ganesh, so a gift that holds their blessing is more than a box of sweets. Here is how to choose a Diwali gift box for family, friends and relatives.

Why a devotional gift works at Diwali

On Diwali night, families perform Lakshmi–Ganesh puja at home. A gold and silver plated box with the deities, shubh–labh plaques and charan paduka gives the receiver something to keep at the puja space all year, not something that is used up in a week.

Because the box is complete, it also suits households that already have a puja corner: it can sit beside the existing murtis, or be gifted as the first piece for a new home.

Which design to choose

  • Classique – the classic Lakshmi · Ganesh and Lakshmi · Ganesh · Saraswati boxes. A safe choice for almost every home.
  • Deluxe – a carved presentation box with a richer finish, for elders and close family.
  • Humsafar – a charan paduka box for nearly every deity, such as Kuber · Lakshmi or Ashtalakshmi, when you know the family's ishta devata.
  • Yantram – a compact 4 × 4 × 2 in keepsake, good for larger gift lists.

Gifting to many people at once

If you are gifting to a large circle of relatives, customers or staff, a single design in bulk is the simplest. Bulk orders start at 120 pieces per design, and a logo or name can be added to the box.

Order a sample first: you can check the finish and packaging yourself. The sample cost is adjusted against your bulk order.

Questions answered

What size are the Diwali gift boxes?

Most boxes are 9 × 4 × 2 inches (L × W × H). Yantram boxes are 4 × 4 × 2 inches and corporate boxes are 9 × 4 × 5 inches.

What is the minimum order for bulk Diwali gifts?

The minimum order quantity is 120 pieces per design. A paid sample is available before you place a bulk order.

How long does delivery take?

We deliver across India. Metro cities usually take 2–3 days and other locations 5–8 days, depending on place and distance.
