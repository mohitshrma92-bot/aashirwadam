Gifting guide

Housewarming gifts for a new beginning.

A new home starts with a puja. A devotional gift box is one of the most fitting housewarming and Griha Pravesh gifts, because it gives the family something sacred for their first day.

Choosing the deity

Lakshmi · Ganesh is the most common choice for a new home: prosperity and auspicious beginnings.

Ram Parivar or Radha Krishna suit families who want a gift for the home temple that speaks of harmony.

Kuber · Lakshmi is chosen for wealth and abundance. If you know the family's ishta devata, the Humsafar collection has a box for nearly every deity.

What makes a good housewarming gift box

A good gift box is easy to place: it should look complete on a shelf or in a puja unit. Our boxes are 9 × 4 × 2 inches, small enough for a modern flat and finished well enough to display.

Gifting for a group or a builder project

If you are a builder, interior designer or developer gifting to many families at handover, you can order in bulk (minimum 120 pieces per design), with a name or logo on the box. Order a sample first and the cost is adjusted against the bulk order.
