Privacy Policy.

How we handle your information when you use this website, message us on WhatsApp or order a sample.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Aashirwadam Creations ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you visit aashirwadam.in (the "website"). By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you order a sample or enquire, you send us your name, phone number, delivery address and design preferences through WhatsApp, and sometimes your company logo. The website itself has no accounts and takes no payments.
  • Information collected automatically. When you browse the website, tools such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel collect technical and usage data, such as the pages you view, the buttons you tap, your device and browser type, approximate location and IP address.
  • Information from WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. If you contact us on these platforms, we see what you send us, subject to those platforms' own policies.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to enquiries, prepare quotes and fulfil sample and bulk orders, including delivery.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To show our products to people who visited the website, and to measure our advertising on Facebook, Instagram and Google.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

3. Cookies and tracking tools

We use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel. When you first visit, a notice asks you to accept or decline. If you decline, we stop sending data to these tools from your browser. You can change your choice by clearing this website's data in your browser. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, use Google's browser add-on for opting out of Analytics, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

4. Who we share information with

  • Courier and delivery partners, who receive your name, phone number and address so that they can deliver your order.
  • Service providers such as Google and Meta, which process analytics and advertising data under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, where the law requires.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep information

We keep order and enquiry details only for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Analytics data is kept according to the settings of the tool providers.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, unless we must keep it by law. Write to us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send card or bank passwords or OTPs in any chat.

8. Children

The website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Links to other websites

The website links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and other services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
